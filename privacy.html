FieldPhoto Cam Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 24, 2026

FieldPhoto Cam is designed to help users capture, organize, label, measure, and share photos and videos for field documentation.

Local Storage

FieldPhoto Cam stores captured photos, videos, job/address information, areas, comments, measurements, timestamps, overlay preferences, company logo settings, and related media information locally within the app's storage on your device.

FieldPhoto Cam does not currently provide user accounts, FieldPhoto cloud storage, or a FieldPhoto-operated synchronization service.

Camera

FieldPhoto Cam uses your device's camera to capture photos and videos. Camera access is also required for supported measurement features.

Microphone

Microphone access is used only to record audio with videos. FieldPhoto Cam does not request microphone access for photo-only use.

If microphone permission is denied, video may still be recorded without audio.

Apple Photos

Saving additional copies to Apple Photos is optional. You may choose:

  • Off
  • Overlay Only
  • Raw + Overlay

FieldPhoto Cam's primary media storage remains inside the app regardless of this setting.

Normal FieldPhoto Cam operation does not require full access to your Photos library.

Company Logo

If you select a company logo, FieldPhoto Cam stores a local copy within the app's Application Support storage. The logo may be included in Overlay media when you enable that option.

Raw media is not modified with company-logo branding.

Measurement

FieldPhoto Cam may use the device camera and supported augmented-reality capabilities to assist with measurements. Measurement information you choose to include may be rendered into Overlay media and stored with your FieldPhoto content.

Sharing

FieldPhoto Cam does not automatically transmit your job media to outside services.

When you choose to share photos, videos, folders, or ZIP archives, FieldPhoto Cam uses the native iOS sharing system. The destination you select, such as Messages, Mail, WhatsApp, cloud storage, or another application, is responsible for its own privacy practices.

Deleting Media

Deleting media within FieldPhoto Cam removes the applicable copies stored in FieldPhoto Cam's app storage.

Copies previously exported to Apple Photos or shared with another application are separate and are not automatically deleted when FieldPhoto Cam copies are removed.

App Removal

Uninstalling FieldPhoto Cam removes data stored within the app's local container. Media previously exported or shared outside FieldPhoto Cam may remain in those locations.

Device and iCloud backup behavior is controlled by Apple and the user's device settings.

Tracking, Advertising, and Analytics

FieldPhoto Cam does not currently contain first-party advertising, behavioral tracking, or intentionally integrated analytics services.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as FieldPhoto Cam gains new functionality. Material changes that affect how information is handled will be reflected in an updated policy.

Contact

Questions regarding this Privacy Policy may be sent to: fieldphotocam@gmail.com
