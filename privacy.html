FieldPhoto Cam Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

FieldPhoto Cam is designed to help users capture, organize, label, annotate, measure, export, and share photos and videos for field documentation. This policy explains the information processed by FieldPhoto Cam and, for the Android Measure feature, by Google Play Services for AR (ARCore).

Information You Provide and Create

Depending on how you use FieldPhoto Cam, the app may process:

  • photos and videos you capture;
  • audio recorded with videos when microphone permission and microphone hardware are available;
  • project, job, address, area, comment, and configurable custom-field text;
  • timestamps, overlay labels, colors, positions, font choices, and other overlay preferences;
  • measurements you create;
  • annotation text, shapes, arrows, lines, freehand marks, colors, sizes, and positions;
  • a company logo image you explicitly select; and
  • media information and camera or EXIF metadata associated with your files.

Local Storage

On Android, Raw, Overlay, and Annotated media, annotation metadata, capture records, preferences, and the app-managed copy of a selected company logo are stored in FieldPhoto Cam's private app sandbox. Other apps cannot normally access this sandbox.

On iOS, FieldPhoto Cam stores app-managed content locally in its app container. A selected company logo is stored in the app's Application Support storage.

FieldPhoto Cam does not provide user accounts, FieldPhoto-operated cloud storage, or a FieldPhoto-operated synchronization service.

Camera

FieldPhoto Cam uses your device's camera to capture photos and videos. Camera access is also required for supported augmented-reality Measure features.

Microphone

Microphone access is used only to record audio with videos. FieldPhoto Cam does not request microphone access for photo-only use.

If microphone permission is denied, video may still be recorded without audio. On supported Android devices, silent video is also available when microphone hardware is unavailable.

Raw, Overlay, and Annotated Media on Android

On Android, FieldPhoto Cam stores authoritative Raw and Overlay masters as separate files in the app's private sandbox. Creating an Overlay does not overwrite the Raw master. Configurable overlay metadata can include up to five text fields, user-entered values, field labels, independent colors, corner placement, timestamp settings, font choice, measurements, and an enabled company logo.

Photo Annotation stores annotation instructions and a separate flattened Annotated JPEG derivative. Annotation saves are additive and do not write to the selected Raw or Overlay source. Saving a newer annotation revision can replace the prior saved annotation metadata and derivative for that source; the Raw or Overlay source remains unchanged.

Apple Photos

Saving additional copies to Apple Photos is optional. You may choose:

  • Off
  • Overlay Only
  • Raw + Overlay

FieldPhoto Cam's primary media storage remains inside the app regardless of this setting.

Normal FieldPhoto Cam operation does not require full access to your Photos library.

Android Gallery

On Android 10 and newer, Save to Gallery uses Android MediaStore to create a separate copy only after you request it. This does not modify or move the app's authoritative master, and FieldPhoto Cam does not request broad storage access for this feature. Android 8 and 9 do not support FieldPhoto Cam's current Save to Gallery implementation.

Copies saved to Android Gallery or Apple Photos are controlled by that destination and are not automatically deleted when the FieldPhoto Cam copy is deleted.

Company Logo

If you select a company logo, FieldPhoto Cam reads only the image you choose and stores an app-managed local copy. On iOS, this copy is stored in the app's Application Support storage. The logo may be included in Overlay media when you enable that option.

Raw media is not modified with company-logo branding. Removing the logo in FieldPhoto Cam removes the app-managed copy; copies already rendered into Overlay media remain part of those media files.

Measure and Google Play Services for AR on Android

FieldPhoto Cam may use the device camera and supported augmented-reality capabilities to assist with measurements. Measurement information you choose to include may be rendered into Overlay media and stored with your FieldPhoto content.

On Android, Measure uses Google Play Services for AR (ARCore), provided by Google. Use of ARCore is subject to the Google Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.

According to Google's ARCore Data Safety guidance, ARCore automatically collects the following data regardless of which ARCore APIs an app uses:

  • a Google Account identifier when the user is signed in, or a device-specific identifier when the user is not signed in;
  • performance and diagnostic information about the app, ARCore, and the device; and
  • ARCore API usage and app-activity information.

Google states that this collection is required, is used for analytics, operation, diagnostics, and improving AR experiences, is not shared by Google with third parties, and is encrypted in transit on supported devices that originally shipped with Google Play. FieldPhoto Cam's Android Measure feature does not use ARCore Cloud Anchors or the ARCore Geospatial API. It therefore does not intentionally invoke the additional precise-location, video, or Cloud Anchor collection that Google associates with those APIs.

See Google's ARCore Data Safety guidance. Measurements you choose to keep are stored locally by FieldPhoto Cam and may be included in Overlay media. FieldPhoto Cam does not operate a server that receives those measurements.

Sharing

FieldPhoto Cam does not automatically transmit your job media to a FieldPhoto server.

When you choose Share or ZIP export on Android, FieldPhoto Cam creates copies in its private cache and opens the Android system share chooser. Only the destination you select receives access to those copies. The selected destination—such as messaging, email, cloud storage, or another app—has its own privacy practices.

Temporary Android Share and ZIP staging copies are isolated from the authoritative Raw, Overlay, and Annotated files. Failed preparations are removed immediately. Abandoned staging directories are eligible for automatic removal after seven days when FieldPhoto Cam next performs cleanup.

On iOS, when you choose to share photos, videos, folders, or ZIP archives, FieldPhoto Cam uses the native iOS sharing system. The destination you select, such as Messages, Mail, WhatsApp, cloud storage, or another application, is responsible for its own privacy practices.

EXIF and Camera Metadata

Media files may contain capture time and camera or device metadata. When FieldPhoto Cam creates an Annotated JPEG on Android, it preserves a limited set of available source EXIF fields, including capture date and time, device make and model, exposure information, focal length, and GPS EXIF fields if those fields are already present in the source image. FieldPhoto Cam does not request Android location permission and does not independently add GPS coordinates.

A user-directed Gallery save, Photos save, Share, or ZIP export can include metadata already contained in the exported file.

Backup and Device Transfer

On Android, FieldPhoto Cam excludes its data from Android cloud backup. On Android versions that support separate device-transfer rules, Android device-to-device transfer may transfer the private FieldPhoto files directory to your replacement device. That directory can include app-managed masters, annotation metadata and derivatives, capture records, and company-logo data. This is a user-controlled Android device migration, not a FieldPhoto cloud service.

On Android 11 and earlier, where cloud backup and device transfer cannot be separately configured, FieldPhoto Cam excludes its app data from both mechanisms. On iOS, device and iCloud backup behavior is controlled by Apple and your device settings.

Retention and Deletion

FieldPhoto Cam retains app-managed content until you delete the applicable media or project in the app, remove a selected company logo, clear the app's data through system settings, or uninstall the app. On Android, deleting a Raw or Overlay source also removes annotation metadata and the Annotated derivative associated with that source. Deleting an entire project removes that project's app-managed content.

Uninstalling FieldPhoto Cam or clearing its app data removes data in the app's private container, subject to the operating system's deletion and device-transfer behavior. Copies previously saved to Android Gallery, Apple Photos, transferred to a new device, or shared with another application are separate and must be managed in those destinations.

First-Party Analytics, Advertising, and Uploads

FieldPhoto Cam itself does not contain a first-party analytics or telemetry system, advertising, behavioral tracking, Firebase, Sentry, crash-report uploading, a first-party HTTP client, or an automatic user-data upload service. FieldPhoto Cam does not operate a cloud backend that receives user-created content.

This is distinct from the automatic data collection performed by Google Play Services for AR when the Android Measure feature uses ARCore, as described above. Opening a Privacy, Support, Google Terms, Google Privacy, or ARCore guidance link also launches your browser, whose data practices are separate from FieldPhoto Cam.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as FieldPhoto Cam gains new functionality. Material changes that affect how information is handled will be reflected in an updated policy.
