Reporting a Problem

When reporting an issue, including the following information can help us troubleshoot it:

  • Your FieldPhoto Cam version
  • Your iPhone model
  • Your iOS version
  • A short description of what happened
  • The steps that caused the problem, if known

Please do not email confidential client photos, videos, addresses, or other sensitive job information unless it is necessary to troubleshoot the issue and you are authorized to share it.
